+The Three Seats

One project. Three disciplines. Persistent friction.

Owners, architects, and contractors make decisions that affect each other every day — yet few possess deep working knowledge across all three. OAC exists to make collaboration across the disciplines legible, practical, and advisable.

Owners

Rising costs force constant trade-offs between quality and control. Development strategy, capital, entitlements, and risk — seen from the seat that pays for it all.

Architects

Escalating code complexity, compressed fees, and pressure to cut design to the bone. Design intent meeting construction reality.

Contractors

Procurement delays, scoping complexity, and cost increases — often with the least control over decisions made upstream. Delivery is where every gap surfaces.

If you read OAC, you will spot collaborative opportunities between the disciplines and better navigate the complexities of your own.
