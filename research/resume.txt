The most consequential decisions in real estate development happen at the intersection of architecture, construction, and capital - and most professionals operate fluently in only one of those worlds. Salvatore Raffone operates across all three. Over 25 years of practice spanning global institutional design, ground-up development, and complex construction management, he has developed a rare cross-disciplinary fluency that allows him to see what specialists miss, ask the questions others aren't positioned to ask, and build the bridges that get projects built.
This perspective is formalized in the Owner/Architect/Contractor framework - a risk-mitigation philosophy grounded in the conviction that the delivery of the built environment depends on genuine collaboration across siloed roles. Sal brings this philosophy to every project, every advisory engagement, and every classroom.
